Privacy

Atlas is a free geography flashcard site. It works without an account and stores your progress only in your own browser.

If you sign in with Google

We receive your Google account ID, email address, name and profile picture. We use them only to identify you and to sync your flashcard progress (which cards you have seen, how you graded them, and when) between your devices. Nothing else is read from your Google account.

What we store

Your account details and your review history, on Cloudflare's infrastructure. We do not sell or share this data, run ads, or use third-party analytics.

Deleting your data

Email ethan@m2g2.com from the address you signed in with and your account and all synced progress will be deleted.
